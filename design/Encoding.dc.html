2  THE WEEK CELL
a whole week of one lane in ~6px of width — shown at true size, then at 3×
1  STACKED   CHOSEN
Height is how much, the solid part is what enlarged you. Reads at 6px, no up-is-good axis. Cost: the faded band is easy to miss in a glance across a year.
2  DIVERGING
The most legible of the three — a bad stretch is unmissable. Cost: it makes diminishing a negative number, which the README rules out. Here to be rejected on purpose, not by accident.
3  VOLUME ONLY
The year view answers only “where did it go”; valence waits until you tap a week. Cleanest at 6px, and it refuses to average a body-level judgement over seven days. Cost: the second question goes missing from the payoff view.
